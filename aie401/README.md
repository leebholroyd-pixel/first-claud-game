# AIE401 Artificial Intelligence for Educators, Workshop 1

Interactive resources for Workshop 1, Wednesday 18 November 2026, Blackpool School of Computing, Blackpool and The Fylde College.

Open `index.html`, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## What is here
* Breakout Room Companion, the three breakout task cards with role spinner, step timers and room notes
* AI Bingo, the keynote icebreaker
* AI or Not?, Myth or Reality? and Hallucination Hunter, games
* Prompt Lab, prompt builder and Upgrade Challenge
* Traffic Light Sort, AI in assessment
* Workshop 1 Recap, flashcards, knowledge check, glossary, reflection and directed study tracker
* Session Timer, for producers
* Downloads, the Word and PowerPoint resource pack

All learner names, answers and scenarios are fictional. Progress is saved only in the visitor's browser.
